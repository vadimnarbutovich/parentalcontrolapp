Privacy policy

Draft — not approved for publication. Updated October 20, 2026. Pending final developer review.

ParentalControl, developed by Vadim Narbutovich, helps parents manage their child’s app use from a linked phone. This policy explains how information is used to provide these features.

1. Linking devices and providing the service

The server stores settings, tasks and rewards, time balances and usage summaries, and the child’s latest requested location. It also processes technical device identifiers, pairing and PIN verification data, and commands with their delivery status so the linked phones can communicate. Pairing does not require an account with your name or email address.

2. The child’s location

When you request your child’s location, their phone sends coordinates, accuracy and an update time to the server for display on your linked phone. The latest location is stored for this purpose. Background requests require “Always” location access on the child’s phone. You can change this permission in iOS Settings.

3. How the app works

With the required permissions, ParentalControl manages access to selected apps on the child’s phone. Settings are stored locally and relevant information is synchronized between linked devices. Permissions can be managed in iOS Settings; disabling them may limit the corresponding features.

4. Analytics and supporting services

We use AppMetrica to understand how the app works and improve it. It processes technical information about the app and device, technical identifiers, usage events and purchase-related events. The app does not ask you to provide your name or contact details for analytics. Technical identifiers can distinguish app installations. About AppMetrica data processing.

Third-party infrastructure supports hosting, notifications and subscription management. These services process the information needed for their functions, including device identifiers and purchase status. Processing may take place outside your country.

5. Children and parental involvement

The child’s phone should be set up and managed by a parent or legal guardian, who should explain the controls and location feature to the child. Any consent required by applicable law must be obtained before use.

6. Retention, unlinking and deletion

Settings, tasks, location and technical records are retained to provide synchronization and app features. Unlinking devices initiates deletion of family-linked records; device registration records remain available for reconnection. Removing the app does not by itself delete all server or service-provider data. Contact us to request access to or deletion of your data.

7. Support and this website

If you contact support, we use your email address and message to respond. This website has no analytics scripts or cookies set by its own code. The hosting provider may process connection data.

8. Contact

For questions about the app or your data, email vadim.narb@yandex.com.
